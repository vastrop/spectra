# Supernova — SN types Ia / II / Ib / Ic (no MK type)

## Class: an explosion, not a spectral class
A supernova has no MK type and no Pickles reference: it is the destruction of a star, and its spectrum is the ejecta, not a photosphere in equilibrium. Two physically unrelated events share the name — a thermonuclear detonation of a white dwarf (**Type Ia**), and the core collapse of a massive star (**Types II, Ib, Ic**) — and the whole point of the classification below is to tell them apart, since the letter names are historical and describe only what the spectrum shows.

The ejecta expand at 10,000-15,000 km/s. Every feature is therefore **enormously broad — 200-300 Å at Hα** — and typically a P Cygni profile: a broad emission hump with a blueshifted absorption trough on its blue side. This is the one class of object where low resolution costs you nothing: a slitless spectrum resolves these features perfectly well, and the classification below is genuinely within reach of an amateur frame.

**The annotation ticks will not land on the troughs.** The table gives *rest* wavelengths, but the absorption you actually measure is blueshifted by the expansion velocity — Si II 6355 Å is observed near 6100-6150 Å. Expect roughly a 200 Å blueward offset near maximum light, shrinking as the ejecta slow.

## This type: the decision tree
Work it in this order — it is how SNe are actually typed:

1. **Is Hα there?** A broad Hα P Cygni profile means hydrogen in the envelope: **Type II**, core collapse of a star that kept its hydrogen. Sub-cases: *IIP* (a months-long plateau in the light curve, the commonest), *IIn* (superimposed *narrow* emission lines — the ejecta are lighting up dense circumstellar material shed before the explosion; narrow lines on a broad-lined spectrum are the giveaway), *IIb* (hydrogen visible early, then fading as helium takes over — a transitional object).
2. **No hydrogen — is Si II there?** A deep, broad absorption near **6100-6150 Å** (rest 6355 Å) is the defining mark of **Type Ia**, the thermonuclear white-dwarf explosion used as the cosmological standard candle. Near maximum light it is accompanied by the "S II W" — two absorptions near 5454 and 5640 Å forming a W-shaped notch — which is a Ia confirmation and nothing else's.
3. **No hydrogen, no silicon — is He I there?** He I 5876 (and 6678, 7065) in a hydrogen-free spectrum means **Type Ib**: core collapse of a star stripped of its hydrogen envelope.
4. **Neither hydrogen, silicon, nor helium** → **Type Ic**: stripped of hydrogen *and* helium. Look for O I 7774 and the broad Ca II features; these are the most featureless of the four early on, and the class connected to long gamma-ray bursts.

**Epoch matters as much as type.** The tree above applies near maximum light. Weeks later all types enter a *nebular* phase: the continuum collapses, and emission from iron, cobalt and (in core-collapse events) oxygen takes over as the ejecta become transparent and radioactive decay powers the light. A late spectrum will not read cleanly on the tree.

Ca II H & K and the Ca II infrared triplet are broad and strong in most types — they confirm you are looking at a supernova, but they discriminate nothing. Do not type on calcium.

## Features
Rest wavelengths. Absorption troughs sit ~150-250 Å blueward of these near maximum light; all features are 100-300 Å wide.

| Wavelength (Å) | Feature | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 3934-3968 | Ca II H & K | broad, strong in most types; confirms, discriminates nothing |
| 4000-4500 | Fe-group blanketing | the "iron curtain" — a forest of broad, blended absorption |
| 5169 | Fe II | broad; strengthens through the first weeks |
| 5454 | S II | left arm of the "S II W" — Type Ia near maximum |
| 5640 | S II | right arm of the "S II W" — Type Ia near maximum |
| 5876 | He I | **Type Ib** marker in a hydrogen-free spectrum |
| 6355 | Si II | **Type Ia** marker — observed near 6100-6150 Å (blueshifted) |
| 6563 | Hα | **Type II** marker — broad P Cygni; absent in Ia/Ib/Ic |
| 7774 | O I | prominent in Type Ic; present in core-collapse events |
| 8498-8662 | Ca II triplet | broad, most types; confirms, discriminates nothing |
